About us

A local mechanic who shows up.

Berkshire County Ready Automotive is a locally owned repair business based in Pittsfield. Our owner has spent 10 years working on cars, and started this business to make repairs easier for the people around here.

Instead of towing your car to a shop and waiting days, we bring the tools to you. For work that's better done off the road, you can drop your car at our Bartlett Ave location for same-day service.

We're a bilingual business. Call, text or message us on WhatsApp in English or Spanish.
